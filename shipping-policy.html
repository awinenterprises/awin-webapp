Shipping & Delivery Policy

Awin Enterprises ships orders across India from Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh.

Delivery charges

  • Orders below ₹1,000: ₹50 flat shipping.
  • Orders of ₹1,000 or more: free shipping.

Dispatch and delivery

We pack and dispatch orders within 1–2 working days of payment confirmation. Delivery typically takes 3–7 working days, depending on your delivery pincode. You can check the delivery estimate on the product page.

Order tracking

We send tracking updates by WhatsApp once your order ships.
